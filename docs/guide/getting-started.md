# First steps

Sheet Tailor has two ways to make the sheet yours. You can use both in the same world, and even on the same character.

| | Customize the system's sheet | Freeform Sheet |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starting point | The sheet you already use | A complete sheet generated by the module |
| What you change | Position, size and colors of the blocks, artwork behind | Everything: each field goes wherever you want, on as many pages as you want |
| How it works | It is the system's own sheet | The system's rolls and items; the original sheet stays in the last tab |
| Best for | People who like the system sheet and only want to adjust it | People with a drawn sheet, or who want to build their own |

## Path 1: customize the sheet you already use

1. Open the character with its usual sheet.
2. Click **Customize sheet** at the top of the window. On the D&D 5e sheet, it is in the menu at the top of the window, behind the three-dot button.
3. Click a block of the sheet to select it and drag it. The corner handle changes its size.
4. In the **Artwork** section of the panel, pick an image, GIF or video to go behind the sheet.
5. Click **Save customization**.

Everyone who can open the sheet sees the customization. The full guide is in [Customize sheet](/guide/customize-sheet).

## Path 2: the Freeform Sheet

1. At the top of the character sheet, click **Sheet** (on D&D 5e, **Configure Sheet**, inside the three-dot menu).
2. In **This Sheet**, choose **Freeform Sheet (custom layout)** and save.
3. The sheet opens ready to play, with the same tabs as the system sheet. The last tab, **Standard Sheet**, is the original sheet, working as usual.
4. To change the look, click the ruler and pencil button, **Edit layout**, at the top of the window.
5. In **Background and size**, choose the page image and drag the elements onto the artwork.
6. Click **Save layout**.

![The layout editor open on a drawn FateX sheet](/media/05-design-fatex.gif)

To learn more, start with the [Freeform Sheet overview](/guide/freeform-sheet).

## Who can do what

- Each player customizes and builds the sheets of the characters they own. The GM can change all of them.
- The GM can keep editing to themselves: in **Game Settings** → **Configure Settings** → **Sheet Tailor**, set **Who can edit sheet layouts** to **GMs only**. Players keep using their sheets as usual.
- To upload images from their own computer, a player needs permission to use Foundry's file browser. Without it, they can paste an image address (URL).

More details in [Settings and permissions](/guide/settings).

# Animated sheets

Any Sheet Tailor background can be animated: a GIF or a WebM or MP4 video works in place of an image. Fields, boxes and rolls keep working on top of the animation.

![An animated FateX sheet in noir style, with neon and rain](/media/08-showcase-noir.webp)

## Where to use animation

| Place | How |
| --- | --- |
| Background of a Freeform Sheet page | In **Background and size**, choose the GIF or video in **Image, GIF or video**. |
| Behind the system sheet | In the customizer, **Artwork** section, in **Image, GIF or video behind the sheet**. |
| An element of the page | An **Image** element set to **Fixed image** accepts video, to animate just one part, such as a crest or a flame. |

Videos play on their own, in a loop and without sound, for everyone who opens the sheet.

![An animated Pathfinder 2e sheet for a sorcerer, with a starry sky](/media/10-showcase-sorcerer.webp)

## How to build an animated sheet

1. **Draw the still artwork first**, with the spaces where the fields will go. That is where you will place the elements.
2. **Animate only what does not get in the way of reading**: glows, rain, smoke, a flickering neon. Motion behind a number tires the eyes.
3. **Export a short loop** whose last frame joins the first. 5 to 10 seconds is usually enough.
4. **Use the still artwork to build the layout** and swap in the video at the end. With the page at the same size, the elements stay in place.

## Performance

Each open animated sheet is a video playing on each player's computer. To keep the table light:

- **Prefer WebM to GIF.** A GIF is much heavier for the same animation and has fewer colors.
- **Use the page size.** A video bigger than the page is not sharper, only heavier.
- **Keep the file small.** A few megabytes per page are enough for a short loop.
- **Close the sheets you are not using.** The animation only costs while the sheet is open.
